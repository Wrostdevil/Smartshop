## Section 1: Standard Manufacturer Warranty Terms
All brand-new electronic goods, smartphones, laptops, audio peripherals, and home appliances sold on the ShopSmart platform carry genuine manufacturer warranties:
1. **Smartphones & Cellular Devices**: Brand-new smartphones include a standard manufacturer warranty of **1 year (12 months)** covering internal motherboard defects, speaker/mic failures, and manufacturing faults from the date printed on the purchase tax invoice.
2. **Bundled Accessories**: In-box charging adapters, cables, and earphones carry a **6-month limited warranty**.
3. **Laptops & Computers**: All standard laptops include a 1-year limited parts and labor manufacturer warranty.

## Section 2: ShopSmart Protect (Extended Warranty Plans)
Customers may purchase an optional ShopSmart Protect extended warranty for up to an additional 2 years beyond the factory warranty:
- Extended warranty plans must be added during purchase checkout or within 15 calendar days of order delivery.
- Protect plans cover mechanical and electrical breakdown under normal everyday usage.

## Section 3: Warranty Exclusions and Void Conditions
The manufacturer warranty and ShopSmart Protect coverage are strictly voided by:
- Accidental drops, physical impact fractures, cracked screen glass, or casing deformation (unless dedicated Accidental Damage Protection was separately bought).
- Water or liquid ingress, corrosion, high-humidity damage, or burn marks from power surges.
- Tampered serial number or IMEI tags.
- Disassembly, unauthorized repair attempts, or firmware rooting.

## Section 4: How to Initiate a Warranty Claim
1. Contact the authorized brand service center listed on your warranty registration card with a copy of your ShopSmart invoice.
2. Alternatively, request ShopSmart Concierge Warranty Pickup through the customer app; ShopSmart couriers will pick up the device and transport it to the certified service center for evaluation.
