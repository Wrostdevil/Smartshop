## Section 1: Refund Processing Timeline and Method
Once a returned item is received at the ShopSmart fulfillment hub, our quality control team completes the diagnostic inspection within 1 to 2 business days. Following inspection approval:
1. **UPI and Net Banking**: Refunds to UPI IDs and direct bank accounts take between 3 to 5 business days to reflect in the customer's account.
2. **Credit and Debit Cards**: Card refunds take between 5 to 7 business days depending on the card-issuing bank's settlement cycle.
3. **ShopSmart Wallet and Store Credits**: Refunds to ShopSmart digital wallet are processed instantly within 24 hours of return approval.
4. **Cash on Delivery (COD)**: For COD orders, refunds are credited via NEFT transfer to a customer-verified bank account within 3 to 5 business days, or instantly as ShopSmart store credit.

## Section 2: Non-Refundable Fees and Charges
The following ancillary charges are non-refundable once the associated services have been rendered:
- Expedited shipping fees (Next-Day or Same-Day delivery surcharges).
- Gift packaging and customized greeting card fees.
- Home installation and white-glove assembly charges.

## Section 3: Restocking Fees and Deductions
- Standard consumer electronics (smartphones, consumer laptops, audio accessories) incur zero restocking fees if returned within the policy guidelines.
- Commercial-grade appliances, enterprise server hardware, and open-box specialized imaging equipment are subject to a mandatory 15% restocking fee deducted from the final refund amount.

## Section 4: Refund Escalations and Disputed Transactions
If a customer does not receive their refund within 10 business days following return acceptance, they may escalate through ShopSmart Helpdesk with their unique Return Authorization Number (RAN). ShopSmart finance specialists investigate and resolve payment provider settlement delays within 48 business hours.
