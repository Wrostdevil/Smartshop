## Section 1: Overview of ShopSmart Club (Formerly Prime Tier)
ShopSmart Club (referenced as ShopSmart Prime or Club Membership) is an exclusive annual loyalty program designed to deliver prioritized logistics, elevated savings, and premium concierge service to verified members across Region IN.

## Section 2: Unlimited Free Delivery Benefits
1. **Free Standard Delivery**: ShopSmart Club members receive **100% free standard delivery on all eligible orders with NO minimum purchase requirement**. The standard ₹499 minimum cart value threshold is completely waived.
2. **Free Express Delivery**: Active members receive free 1-to-2 day Express Delivery on eligible metro catalog items, waiving the typical ₹99 express surcharge.
3. **Discounted Same-Day Shipping**: Same-Day Delivery is available to members for a discounted rate of ₹49 (instead of ₹149 standard charge).

## Section 3: Priority Support and Extended Returns
1. **24/7 Dedicated Concierge Support**: Members receive access to a dedicated direct phone line and priority chat queue, with an average response time of under 30 seconds.
2. **Extended Return Grace Window**: Club members enjoy a 3-day grace period on standard return deadlines (17 days for standard electronics rather than 14 days).

## Section 4: Exclusive Member Pricing and Early Flash-Sale Access
- Members get 24-hour early access to annual promotional events, festive sales, and limited-stock hardware drops.
- Additional 5% instant cashback on selected ShopSmart private label brands.

## Section 5: Subscription Pricing, Renewal, and Cancellation
- Annual subscription fee is ₹999 per year; monthly plan option is ₹99 per month.
- Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time via the Account Settings dashboard. If no member benefits (such as free delivery or sale access) were utilized during the billing cycle, a 100% refund of the membership fee will be processed within 5 business days.
