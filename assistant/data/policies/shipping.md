## Section 1: Delivery Options and Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
ShopSmart partners with premier courier logistics providers across Region IN to deliver packages safely:
1. **Standard Delivery**: Estimated delivery timeline is 3 to 5 business days for Tier-1 and Tier-2 metropolitan locations. Tier-3 and rural pincodes require 5 to 7 business days.
2. **Express Delivery**: Estimated delivery timeline is 1 to 2 business days in select urban centers (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata).
3. **Same-Day Delivery**: Available for eligible items ordered before 11:00 AM local time within designated metropolitan limits.

## Section 2: Delivery Charges and Free Shipping Thresholds
- **Standard Shipping Fee**: Free delivery is automatically granted on all standard cart orders exceeding ₹499. Orders below ₹499 incur a flat delivery fee of ₹49.
- **Express Shipping Surcharge**: A flat fee of ₹99 applies to all express orders unless covered by an active ShopSmart Club membership.
- **Heavy Cargo Handling**: Oversized freight (refrigerators, treadmills, large TVs) incurs a supplementary ground transit fee of ₹350.

## Section 3: Order Cancellation Rules Before and After Shipment
1. **Pre-Shipment Cancellation**: Customers can cancel an order free of charge at any moment prior to order dispatch from the fulfillment warehouse via the ShopSmart app or website.
2. **Post-Shipment Cancellation Policy**: Once an order has been marked as shipped and handed over to the courier partner, it **CANNOT be cancelled directly online**.
3. **Procedure for Shipped Orders**: To reject an unwanted order that has already shipped, the customer must refuse physical delivery at the doorstep when contacted by the delivery courier, or accept delivery and subsequently initiate a standard return request in compliance with our Returns Policy.

## Section 4: Delivery Rescheduling and Address Changes
- Delivery address corrections are accepted only within 60 minutes of placing the order, provided warehouse packing has not begun.
- Customers may request a delivery reschedule up to 48 hours via the courier SMS tracking portal link.
