# ShopSmart Returns Policy

- **Document ID**: doc-policy-returns-2026
- **Document Title**: ShopSmart Standard Returns Policy
- **Category**: returns
- **Version**: 2026.1
- **Effective Date**: 2026-10-01
- **Region**: IN

---

## Section 1: General Return Window
Customers in Region IN may initiate a return request within 14 calendar days from the date of confirmed delivery for standard electronics, appliances, and general consumer goods. For clothing, footwear, and lifestyle fashion items, an extended return window of 30 calendar days applies. Returns requested after the designated return window will be rejected automatically by the system.

## Section 2: Electronics and Computing Devices (Laptops)
For laptops, desktop computers, tablets, and mobile workstations:
1. **Unopened Products**: Unopened laptops with factory seals intact may be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full refund.
2. **Opened Laptops and Computing Devices**: An opened laptop or computing device can **ONLY** be returned if it has a verified hardware defect, is dead-on-arrival (DOA), or suffered verifiable physical transit damage reported within 7 days of delivery.
3. **Buyer's Remorse Exception**: Opened laptops cannot be returned for change of mind, buyer's remorse, subjective performance dissatisfaction, or preference if the hardware is defect-free.
4. **Diagnostic Verification**: All opened laptop returns are subject to a mandatory technical inspection by a certified ShopSmart technician prior to return approval.

## Section 3: Physical Condition and Packaging Requirements
To qualify for a valid return:
- Products must be returned with all original manufacturer packaging, user manuals, accessories, power adapters, warranty cards, and bundled promotional items.
- The product serial number, IMEI, and barcode labels must be fully intact and match the invoice record.
- Products showing signs of customer-induced physical damage, liquid ingress, software rooting, or unauthorized modification are strictly ineligible for return.

## Section 4: Non-Returnable Product Categories
The following product categories are non-returnable under any circumstance:
- Perishable goods, groceries, and consumable items.
- Digital software licenses, downloadable content, gift vouchers, and gaming cards once activated.
- Personal hygiene and grooming products (e.g., electric shavers, earplugs, toothbrushes) once the outer hygienic seal is unsealed.

## Section 5: Regional Return Policy Discrepancies
- **Region IN**: Standard electronics return window is 14 days. Opened laptop return is restricted exclusively to hardware defects or DOA within 7 days.
- **Region US**: Standard electronics return window is 30 days. Opened laptops in like-new condition may be returned within 30 days subject to a standard 10% restocking fee.
